Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

No, walk-ins are seen in order of arrival. Calling ahead gives you a fixed time and usually no wait.

What should I bring?

Bring any previous reports, your current medicines (or photos of the strips), and a photo ID.

How much is a consultation?

Please call us for current fees. We accept cash, UPI and major cards.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your records are confidential and shared only with your consent. This website does not collect any personal data.
